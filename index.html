Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Retirement Countdown Timer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Retirement Countdown Timer stores all user settings (such as target retirement date, working hours, vacation hours, and background preferences) locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, such as Google Play Services and Google AdMob (for displaying ads in the free version). These third-party providers have their own privacy policies.

3. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@retirementtimer.com
